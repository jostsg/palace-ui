Cards

3-color accent rotation. Hover for lift + shadow.

First Card

Accent 1 (pink). Headings pick up the accent color automatically. Hover to see the lift and hard shadow.

Second Card

Accent 2 (purple). The rotation cycles through all three accent colors on sibling cards.

Third Card

Accent 3 (cyan shadow on hover). Heading uses dark text for accessible contrast.

Fourth Card

Back to accent 1 (pink). The rotation repeats every 3 cards.

Static Card (no lift, keeps its large shadow)

Form Container

Use .card-static for cards that hold forms or content that shouldn't lift on hover; it keeps the large shadow.

Control Row

Inputs, fields and buttons on one line, aligned to the bottom. Inputs grow and share the width; the row wraps when it is narrow. A hint or error for the row goes below it, not inside the field, so the button stays in line with the control.
